Ok, so VFX work isn't always about making shots sparkle.  More often than not, it's about avoiding showing your hand at all.  Most VFX is about removing wires and other rigging, extending a set that doesn't totally fill the frame, or deleting the coffee cup accidently left on a medieval fantasy banquet table.  

Sometimes though, VFX work IS about making things sparkle.  Sometimes it's about adding glowy stuff, or explosions, or splashes of colour and light.  Sometimes, you don't want to hide the magic.
